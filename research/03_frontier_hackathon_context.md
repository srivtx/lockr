## 1. What is the Frontier Hackathon?

**Important clarification:** Frontier is **not** run by Superteam India. It is **Solana's Global Hackathon**, powered by **Colosseum** (the official platform for Solana hackathons). Superteam India promotes it and acts as a regional community hub for Indian builders.

- **Name:** Solana Frontier Hackathon
- **Organizer:** Colosseum (in partnership with the Solana Foundation)
- **Promoted by:** Superteam India and other global Superteam chapters
- **Dates:** April 6 – May 11, 2026
- **Format:** Global, online startup competition (not a traditional 24-72 hour hackathon)
- **Duration:** 5 weeks (an intensive engineering + business sprint)
- **Current Status:** Live now
- **Participants:** 16,805+ builders registered
- **Submissions Due:** May 11, 2026

Colosseum hackathons focus on **startups with viable business models**, not just technical demos. The average winning team size is **above 3 people**, often with a mix of technical and non-technical co-founders.

## 4. General Theme and Focus

- **Theme:** "Build on the frontier." There is **no narrow theme** — the focus is on building **impactful crypto startups on Solana**.
- **Mission:** Grow the number of **full-time founders** shipping products, not just grow total developer count.
- **Philosophy:** Hackathons are treated as the **first product sprint** for a startup, not a bounty-completion event.
- **Builder levels supported:**
  1. **Learners** – experimenting and learning
  2. **Contributors** – building tools, boosting resumes, aiming for grants
  3. **Founders** – using the hackathon to launch a startup (Colosseum's primary focus)

**Key focus areas encouraged:**
- Build products that **enable new markets** only possible with crypto
- Improve existing markets by **bringing them on-chain**
- Solve problems you **personally experience**
- Build in verticals where you have **domain expertise**
- Be **wildly ambitious** — the hackathon is just the start

## 5. Submission Rules and Best Practices

### Eligibility & Project Rules
- **Must be new projects**, but teams can start building up to **2 months before** the hackathon start date
- **Must integrate with Solana** in some capacity (the only hard technical requirement)
- Projects are judged on work completed **during the hackathon period**
- You may submit **multiple projects**
- Both **individuals and teams** can submit (team average for winners is 3+)

### Technical Requirements
- Must have a **working demo on devnet**
- Must provide a **GitHub repo link**
- If your repo is **private**, you must grant access to `submissions@colosseum.org`

### Presentation Requirements
- **Presentation video under 3 minutes** (Loom is recommended over slide decks)
- Submissions are judged heavily on **clarity and storytelling**
- Must include:
  - Team background
  - Product description
  - Why you started building it
  - Market opportunity
  - Go-to-market / initial traction
  - Working demo walkthrough

### Submission Best Practices
- Avoid last-minute rushes
- Define clear team roles
- Do not submit unpolished or broken demos — test end-to-end
- Include all key details (repo links, teammate bios)
- Build in public: create a project Twitter/X account and share progress
- Spend ~4 weeks on engineering, 1 week on the presentation and testing

### Judging Criteria
- Product quality and demo functionality
- Team strength and execution ability
- Business model viability and market opportunity
- Clarity of presentation and storytelling
- Preference for teams who intend to build **full-time**

### Sponsors (Frontier)
- **Primary:** Phantom, Altitude
- **Secondary:** Coinbase, Privy, Metaplex, Reflect, Arcium, World, Raydium, MoonPay
