## 1. Problem Statement / What Needs to Be Built

**Mission:** Build a Payments or Finance application that leverages **stablecoins on Solana**, integrating **Dodo Payments** to offer real-world utility to individuals and/or global businesses.

This is a dedicated prize track for the **Solana Frontier hackathon powered by Colosseum**.

**Inspiration Themes (not mandatory):**
- **Cross-Border Payments for Businesses** — Help SaaS, AI-native, or creator platforms pay contractors and teams globally without wire fees or banking friction.
- **Stablecoin Checkouts** — Merchant-facing checkout flows (POS, QR, payment links) that accept stablecoins for real goods and services.
- **Region-Specific Applications** — Payment infrastructure built around local regulatory, currency, or user constraints.
  - Example: Credible (Cypherpunk 2nd place) built a stablecoin-powered remittance platform for banks, fintechs, and other businesses looking to send money in/out of India.
- **Consumer Remittance & Spending Wallets** — Apps that help individuals send/receive money internationally or spend stablecoins day-to-day.
- **Programmable B2B Finance** — Supply chain payments, invoice factoring, or escrow that settles in seconds instead of days for SMEs underserved by banks.
- **DeFi-native Applications** — Treasury tools, yield on idle/transitory stables, smart accounts for businesses, etc. that bridge DeFi and TradFi using stablecoins.
- **Agentic & Autonomous Payments** — Payment infrastructure for AI agents to transact, get paid, or split revenue without human-in-the-loop approval. Brownie points for also integrating **x402**.

## 2. Requirements and Constraints

- Integrate Dodo Payments in a **meaningful, non-trivial way.**
- Solve a **specific problem for a defined user:** merchant, SaaS founder, migrant worker, logistics operator, etc.
- Showcase how **Solana + stablecoins beat the status quo** for this use case (speed, cost, programmability).
- Demonstrate **early traction** — real users, a live pilot, or transaction volume.
- **Regional Restriction:** Only open for people in **India**.
- Teams also need to **sign up and submit projects for the Global Hackathon on Colosseum** (https://colosseum.com/frontier).
